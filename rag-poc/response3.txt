It applies to both COVID-19 vaccine (C19V) shipments and conventional shipments, and must contain details about the shipped batch. It must be signed by IO Quality as an additional local requirement, per the Certificate of Compliance (CoC) issued, and this signoff must occur prior to GDP release being performed under EU legislation for shipments where the Importer of Records (IOR) is Pfizer Pakistan. FORM 7 is not required for Donation C19V shipments where the IOR is the Pakistani government, and it cannot be used as a substitute for the CoC. The completed FORM 7 template captures fields such as the HS code, product name, batch number, manufacturer's name and address, manufacturing and expiry dates, supplier's name and address, and the quality signoff.
